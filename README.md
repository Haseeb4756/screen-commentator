## 📋 What is screen-commentator?

screen-commentator is a macOS app that captures what is on your screen and adds scrolling comments generated by AI over the video. It helps you highlight key points, add notes, or make your screen recordings more engaging. The app works quietly in the background, creating comment overlays automatically as you work or present.

---

## 🖥️ System Requirements

Before you start, make sure your computer fits these requirements:

- Operating System: macOS 10.15 (Catalina) or newer
- RAM: 4 GB or more
- Disk Space: At least 200 MB free
- Internet Connection: Needed for AI comment generation
- Screen Resolution: Any standard macOS screen resolution

## ⚙️ How to Use screen-commentator

After launching screen-commentator, here is how to start capturing your screen with AI comments.

### Start a New Capture  
Click the **Start Capture** button in the app window. The app will begin recording your screen.

### Watch AI Comments Appear  
As you work, the AI creates comments based on what is visible on your screen. These comments scroll over the video in real-time.

### Pause or Stop Capture  
Use the **Pause** or **Stop** buttons to control your recording. When you stop, the app saves your video with the AI comment overlay.

### View Your Recordings  
Open the app’s **Recordings** section to watch previous videos or export them for sharing.

## 🔧 Settings and Customization

screen-commentator lets you adjust how the comments appear and what is captured.

- **Comment Speed:** Change how fast the comments scroll on screen.
- **Font and Size:** Select the font style and size for comments.
- **Capture Area:** Choose to record the full screen or just a selected window.
- **Comment Topics:** Pick what types of comments to generate, like summarizing content or adding technical notes.
- **Save Location:** Set where recorded videos are stored on your Mac.

These options are in the **Settings** tab in the app.

## 🆘 Troubleshooting

If screen-commentator does not work as expected, try these steps:

- Make sure your macOS version is up to date.
- Check internet connection, since AI comments rely on online processing.
- Restart the app if comments do not appear.
- Verify permissions: the app needs access to screen recording. You can grant this in **System Preferences > Security & Privacy > Screen Recording**.
- Reinstall from the latest `.dmg` if problems persist.
